# Shoji-room pendulum clock

The clock is drawn with the existing inline SVG renderer; no reference photograph is bundled. Its wall anchor, Japanese current-time update and independent audio controls are unchanged.

## Construction references

- Seikosha wooden pendulum clock, seller estimates circa 1955–1964 (Showa 30s): https://kottori.shop-pro.jp/?pid=187956537
  Rounded rectangular case, large cream dial with a metal bezel, separate lower glass aperture and a deep wooden back box. Front and rear photographs reviewed.
- Aichi hand-wound pendulum wall clock, purchased in 1964 (Showa 39) according to the seller: https://korekoremart.base.shop/items/70148693
  Front veneer over a deeper case, rounded lower window, black shaped hands, winding holes, hinges and side latch. Front and side photographs reviewed.

The SVG is an original interpretation of their construction, without manufacturer marks. Case shading follows light from the room's right-hand opening/lower-right lantern; its close wall shadow falls upper-left. The existing night treatment remains in place.
